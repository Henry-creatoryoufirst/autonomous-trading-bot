**What:** Standard Kelly scales position sizes up during winning streaks and down during losing streaks. NVR uses fixed Quarter-Kelly with drawdown circuit breakers but doesn't scale up on winning streaks.

**Where to implement:** `src/core/config/constants.ts` → add `KELLY_WIN_STREAK_SCALAR` constant; apply in `agent-v3.2.ts` `calculatePositionSize()`.
**Complexity:** LOW — 5-8 lines.
**Risk:** LOW — additive to existing system.

### Search 3 — Position Sizing
**Query**: Kelly criterion volatility adjusted cryptocurrency drawdown aware position sizing 2026

**Key Finding**: Best-practice 2026 Kelly production sequence:
1. Estimate raw edge
2. Shrink by base rates and confidence
3. Estimate volatility + covariance with regime stress
4. Compute raw Kelly
5. **Apply drawdown-probability constraint** ← NVR does not have this step
6. Apply liquidity and compliance caps

The bot has steps 1-4 and 6 but lacks a dynamic drawdown-probability constraint in
step 5. Currently uses a static `KELLY_FRACTION = 0.25`. A drawdown brake (if
current drawdown > X%, halve effective Kelly fraction) would add the missing step 5.

**Source**: atlaspeakresearch.com Kelly report, Medium Kelly crypto traders article,
coriva.eu.org Kelly position sizing guide

**Score**:
- Impact: 3/5 — meaningful drawdown protection during severe drawdowns
- Complexity: 2/5 — add 1 constant + 1 conditional in Kelly sizing logic
- Risk: low (only reduces position sizes, conservative direction)
- Priority: 3/2 = **1.5** — below 2.0 threshold

**Implemented**: No (below threshold; trigger conditions also unverifiable)

**Watch List**: If drawdown > 12% is detected (trigger condition), consider adding:
```
DRAWDOWN_KELLY_BRAKE_PCT = 12   // threshold for halving Kelly fraction
DRAWDOWN_KELLY_BRAKE_FACTOR = 0.5  // halve it
```
